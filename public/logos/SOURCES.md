# Institution logos

Official artwork, downloaded once from each institution's own site and self-hosted here (no
hotlinking). Colours and shapes are unchanged; files were only resized to about 2× display size by
`node tools/logos.mjs` (Chromium, high-quality resampling) where the original was much larger.

The logos are trademarks of their owners. They are not covered by this site's licence and are used
only to identify the institutions named next to them (study and past employment), each linking to
the institution's own site.
